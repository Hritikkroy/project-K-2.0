The Garden

There was a garden.

And there was you.

I saw you smiling for the first time and completely lost in the moment.(I still remeber your smile honeyy)

You were wearing that cute red shirt, speaking so softly that somehow everything around you seemed quieter.(You were soo shyy)

And me?

My heart was going absolutely crazy.

I was nervous. (Really nervous) Really Really Really nervous.

"Hey... I think I have a crush on you. And you're cute."

There was just one tiny problem.

I was about two months late.

You already did.

You were in love with me too.

You just couldn't bring yourself to say it.

We finally started dating.

It wasn't perfect.(who cares)

We had beautiful moments, and we had moments that hurt.

Eventually, we broke up.

And for a while, things weren't easy.

There were moments when you were angry with me, moments when it probably felt like we'd reached the end of our story.

But there was one thing I was strangely certain about.

I knew what I wanted.

I didn't know when.

I didn't know how.

But I knew I wasn't ready to let our story end there.

And somehow...

It didn't.
